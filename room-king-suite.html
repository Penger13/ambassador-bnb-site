About This Suite

Our King Suite is our largest accommodation at approximately 450 sq ft, featuring two comfortable queen beds, a separate living room, and a full kitchenette. This suite is perfect for families, groups, or travelers who need extra space.

The suite includes premium bedding, a spacious sitting area, independently controlled HVAC, and a walk-in shower. With two queen beds, it comfortably accommodates up to four guests. Breakfast is not included but can be added for $20 CAD per day.
