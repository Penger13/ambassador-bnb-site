About This Suite

Our Premium Suite is our largest and most luxurious offering at approximately 420 sq ft. It features a separate living room, full kitchenette, and a comfortable king bed with premium linens. The suite features a large balcony perfect for enjoying your morning coffee or an evening glass of wine.

Perfect for extended stays, families, or travellers who want the ultimate in comfort and space. The suite includes a spacious sitting area, independently controlled HVAC, and a walk-in shower. Breakfast is not included but can be added for $20 CAD per day.

Keurig & K-Cups: Each suite includes a Keurig machine with 2 complimentary K-Cups per person, per day included in your stay. Additional K-Cups can be purchased if needed.
