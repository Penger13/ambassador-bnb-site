Find us on the map

266 Ontario Street, Stratford

Tucked between the theatre district and downtown, our 1923 Victorian twin house sits on a quiet, tree-lined street — close enough to walk everywhere, far enough to sleep peacefully.

The four white swans flanking our front steps have welcomed guests for over half a century. You'll know you've arrived when you see them.

Free private parking on-site — a rare luxury in Stratford during festival season. No street parking, no garage fees, no stress after a late evening at the theatre.

Start Your Day Right

Homemade Breakfast, Two Traditions

Every morning, we serve a thoughtfully prepared breakfast featuring one of our two signature menus – we rotate between a classic Canadian spread and a traditional Chinese-style breakfast, so every stay offers something new and delicious.

Both options are prepared fresh each morning with high-quality ingredients. Our breakfast is served in our sunlit dining room, or you can take it out to the garden patio on warm mornings. We cater to dietary preferences – just let us know in advance and we'll do our best to accommodate.

☕ Every breakfast includes a selection of fine teas including our signature Fujian oolong, shipped directly from the oolong tea hometown.

📌 For Inn Next Door guests: Breakfast is not included in the suite rate, but can be added for $20 CAD per day. Just let us know when booking!

Your Stratford Guide

Why Stratford Captivates Visitors

The festival, the swans, the gardens, and the restaurants — there's a reason theatre lovers return to this town year after year.

The Stratford Festival

A Theatrical Tradition Since 1953

What began as a small Shakespearean festival under a tent has grown into one of the world's great repertory companies — presenting a season of twelve or more plays across four stages, from April through October.

The whole town comes alive for it. Sidewalks fill with playgoers between matinees and evening shows, restaurants offer pre-theatre menus, and every B&B and inn becomes part of the experience.

01

Stratford Festival

North America's largest repertory theatre festival, running from April to October with Shakespeare, musicals, and contemporary plays across four stages.

02

Four Theatres, One Town

Festival Theatre, Avon Theatre, Studio Theatre, and Tom Patterson Theatre — all within a compact, walkable downtown core.

03

Beyond the Stage

Pre-show talks, backstage tours, concerts, and the Festival's famous costume and prop collections make every visit unique.
