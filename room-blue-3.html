About This Room

Room 3 · Blue is our most popular room, featuring a private balcony overlooking our beautiful garden. The room includes a comfortable queen bed, premium imported linens, and a seating area with two chairs – perfect for enjoying your morning coffee or evening tea outdoors.

Enjoy the extra space and fresh air from your private balcony. The room also features individually controlled air conditioning, soundproofed walls, and complimentary bathrobes for your comfort.
