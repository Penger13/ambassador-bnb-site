About This Suite

Our Deluxe Suite offers approximately 400 sq ft of comfortable living space with a separate living room, full kitchenette, and a king bed dressed in premium linens. The suite features beautiful garden views that add to its peaceful ambiance.

Perfect for longer stays or travelers who prefer more space and self-catering flexibility. The suite includes a spacious sitting area, independently controlled HVAC, and a walk-in shower. Breakfast is not included but can be added for $20 CAD per day.
