Our Policies

Have a look at our B&B rooms and inn suites availability and our special offers. Should you have a special requirement? We will be glad to provide individualized quotes upon request.

Cancellation Policy

50% of total accommodation payment is required at the time of booking.

  • Cancellation made 21 days in advance – we will refund your deposit.
  • Cancellation made less than 15 days prior – full $50 per night/per room deposit is non-refundable.

When your deposit amount is given, it will indicate that you have read and agreed to our policies and your room will be held in good faith.

No Smoking

Smoking or the burning of candles or incense is not permitted in guest rooms or the indoor common areas of the house.

Smoking is allowed on the balcony or veranda only if other guests give their permission, and only with connecting doors and windows closed. Be sure that smoke does not migrate into your room from those areas.

Pets

Our cat, Chip, resides in our personal section of the house. Although he is not allowed in guest areas, he does not allow other pets.
