About This Room

The Yellow Room is one of our most popular choices – flooded with natural light from its large east‑facing windows. It features a comfortable queen‑sized bed dressed in premium imported linens, a private ensuite with a walk‑in shower, and a plush armchair perfect for reading or planning your day.

All rooms are equipped with individually controlled air conditioning, soundproofed walls for a peaceful night, and complimentary bathrobes. We've thought of every detail so you can relax and enjoy your Stratford escape.

Shared Kitchen Access: All B&B guests have access to our second-floor shared kitchen, which includes a Keurig machine with complimentary K-Cups, a water dispenser, microwave, and a mini fridge. Each room also has its own private mini fridge for your convenience.
